How it works

1

Browse a Scenario...

Search the public library by category, tag, or keyword. Filter by rank to find drills that match your interest or crew.

... Or Create Your Own

Build a custom scenario with your own dispatch text, images, and questions. Run it with your crew, department, or open it up to the whole community .

See every submission, reveal answers, and debrief as a group. Reports track who completed what.
